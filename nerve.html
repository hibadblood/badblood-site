NERVE · Every chat, in the owner's language

A chat on a website is not one thing. NERVE is every conversation we run, in the owner's language, and it has two kinds on purpose: one with AI and one without.

In use

The difference is the bill. Where a plain script does the job, no AI runs and nothing costs anything. AI is turned on only where it earns its keep.

Three ways to talk
01

RETINA, the chat with AI

A chat on a business's own site that answers its customers with AI, from what the business has told it and nothing else. It costs tokens, so it is switched on deliberately.

02

CONTACT LENS, the chat without

Anything that needs no AI: a scripted chat on a site, a step-by-step guide that teaches an owner their own system, a file for checking work. Cheap, disposable, free to keep.
