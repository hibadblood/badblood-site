BAD BLOOD COMPANY

Every picture,
one factory.

Each brand needs its own images, endlessly: a wallpaper drawn from one person's birth chart, a post, a story, a menu card, a thumbnail. They do not need their own machine. PLASMA is the machine; the brand is a pack of words it reads.

01 · The run

Five steps.
Then the image exists.

One path for every brand and every size. Nothing about a brand is written into the path — it arrives as data at step two.

01Take the jobA birth chart, a post idea, a product, a schedule slot. It becomes one numbered job with its variables.
02Build the promptThe brand's pack fills a template: subject, palette, motif, camera, what to avoid. Versioned, testable, never typed twice.
03Ask a modelWhichever image model the pack names. Swapping model is a setting, not a rewrite. Failures fall through to the next one.
04Finish itCrop to every size, place the logo, draw the words with real fonts, check contrast. This half is code, not luck.
05DeliverStored once, sent where it was needed: a chat reply, a scheduled post, a page, a folder.
02 · The answer

One machine.
Every brand keeps its own face.

The same shape as the rest of the house: a closed core, a pack per brand, a few dials, and a bespoke lane for the rest.

Layer 1COREQueue, prompt assembly, provider calls, retries, cost ceiling, the finishing pass, storage, delivery. It knows nothing about any brand and never will.
Layer 2PACKOne folder per brand: the style in words, prompt templates with holes in them, reference frames, the sizes it ships, what must never appear. Text files, no code.
Layer 3DIALSWhat the owner changes without asking anyone: model, quality, how many options per job, the spend ceiling for the day. Everything else is refused and written down.
Layer 4DATAThe variables that make one image unlike the next — a chart, a name, a date, a product, a colour drawn from a person. This is where "unique to you" actually lives.
Layer 5BESPOKEWhen a brand needs something the core cannot express, we write it and charge for it — and the result becomes a new pack version, never a fork.
The one hard rule

Models draw pictures.
Code draws the letters.

No image model spells Thai reliably, and a misspelt word on a customer's wallpaper is worse than no wallpaper. So the model is asked for a clean plate and nothing else. Every word, logo, price and label is drawn afterwards with the real font, at the exact size, by code that can be tested. It is the difference between a picture that looks right and one that is right.

03 · What it feeds

The same factory,
five appetites.

Made for one person

  • A wallpaper from a birth chart. The chart becomes the variables; the image exists once, for the person who asked.
  • A card with someone's name on it. Generated at the moment it is needed, not designed in advance.
  • A proof before the work. Four options, one chosen, the rest thrown away.

Made for a feed

  • Daily posts and stories from a list of subjects, in the brand's face, at every size a platform wants.
  • The brands' own surfaces: covers, menus, thumbnails, the images a back office needs printed on a page.
