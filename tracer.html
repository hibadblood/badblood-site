TRACER · You sent it. Then silence.

You send a proposal and hear nothing. TRACER is a proposal that reports back: who opened it, how long they read, how far they got, and a message the moment it happens.

In build

Named for the medical dye. A tracer is what you put in so you can see where something went. Ours does the same for the files we send.

What it does
01

One link per recipient

Every file goes out as its own tracked link, so we know which person opened it, not just that someone did.

02

Open, read, reach

How long they stayed and how far down they got, not only that the page loaded.

03

A vault for what we send

Client links, PDFs, pitches and quotes are meant to live in one place, each with its open record, so sales work stops being a guess.
