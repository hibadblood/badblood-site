What we believe

Whoever holds the brand's memory holds the brand's work.

I · Memory

One memory per brand.

Voice, colour, products, prices, the customers who come back and what has worked on them. Kept as one memory per brand, never mixed, never sold. It updates itself from every conversation and every approved job.

II · Loop

Closed in one conversation.

The ask, the price, the confirmation, the delivery and the approval happen in one thread. No form, no email, no middleman. Every finished job becomes new memory, so the next one is faster and more the brand.

III · Watch

She never sleeps.

A sales dip, a review that turns, a competitor that moves. IRIS reads it in the background and proposes a move before it is needed. The owner decides. She is the one who stays awake.
