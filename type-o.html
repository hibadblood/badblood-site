01 · The answer

Rigid in one place.
Adjustable in four.

Each kind of difference goes in the layer where it is cheapest to change. When a request fits no layer, we say no in a specific place instead of bending the machine.

Layer 1KERNELNine checks, twelve actions, eight kinds of state. That is the entire language, and it is closed — a new one needs two paying customers.
Layer 2PACKA trade written as configuration: its words, steps, numbers and buttons. 454 lines, zero code. A pack may not add a table, a column or a line of JavaScript — the only thing standing between a pack and a fork.
Layer 3DIALSNine settings the owner changes from a web page. Everything else is refused quietly and written to a log, so a typo can never rewrite a workflow.
Layer 4DATARecipes, price tiers, credit customers, opening balances. Four fifths of "my business is different" lives here — rows, not columns. No engineering at all.
Layer 5BESPOKEConfiguration runs out at the tenth check. Then we write code and charge for it — but the output is a new pack version, never a branch.

04 · How it is used

Five verbs.
Six desks.

Every business we looked at does the same five things to a unit of work, and pays people at the same six desks to do them. TYPE O does the repeatable half of each desk. Judgement stays with people.

What you do with it

01TAKE INA request arrives from LINE, the web, a phone call or a walk-in. It becomes one numbered unit of work — a job, a booking, an order, a parcel — with the customer attached.
02ASSIGNIt goes to a person by the rule you chose: nearest, least busy, whoever owns the customer, or in turn. A timer takes it back if nobody accepts.
03MOVEStaff push it through its states from a phone. Every step is checked before it happens — stock never goes below zero, nothing is paid twice, nothing skips a step.
04COLLECTQR, transfer slip, card, credit. The receipt sends itself. Money owed is a list with names on it, not a memory.
05CLOSE THE DAYAt the hour you set: sales, stock, what is owed, who did what. One message. Nobody compiles it.

The desks it covers

Front deskIntake, confirmations, reminders, “is it ready?” People still greet people.
CoordinatorWho does what next, chasing, rescheduling, the whiteboard. The exceptions still get a phone call.
Admin clerkReceipts, invoices, the daily report, the filing. Gone entirely.
Stock keeperCounts, cost per unit, reorder alerts, what expires first. Someone still opens the boxes.
BookkeeperEvery entry in the ledger, the month’s P&L draft, receivables. The accountant still signs.
Follow-upStatus replies, payment reminders, “where is my order”. Complaints go to a human, every time.
05 · Where it is

It runs. And here
is what does not.

Built and tested

  • One engine, one abstraction. No jobs table, no bookings table, no orders table. Two packs run on it today — a roastery and a vehicle-repair workshop — through one validator.
  • The ledger. Lots, FIFO, FEFO, weighted average, recipes, landed cost — the database itself refuses stock below zero. Costing proven to the satang.
  • Money. QR payment, transfer slips read from the slip's own QR and checked with a vendor, duplicate slips refused, refunds, receivables, tax fields, monthly P&L. Anything unsure goes to a human, never to "paid".
  • In and out. CSV import with a preview before anything is written, idempotent on re-upload; eleven CSV exports including the monthly P&L.
  • Timers, roles, numbers. Sub-minute offer timers on Durable Object alarms; job titles as data per company, gates by capability; utilisation and no-shows computed from real bookings. Every route replayed as a second company to prove nothing leaks. 521 tests.

Not built yet

  • Slip vendors are tested against fixtures, not a live key yet; the first thirty real slips are the proof. The second vendor slot is wired but idle.
  • Customer slip photos from LINE do not reach the checker yet; today a slip is uploaded on the web.
  • Receivable reminders have a schedule but no LINE push or button.
  • A finished booking leaves the utilisation count the moment its work completes — the fix is one primitive change, proposed and not yet made.
  • Not started: cached KPI snapshots, rich menus per role, public token pages, a bank-statement importer.
